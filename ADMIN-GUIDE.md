# ETA Website – Admin Guide (for Dustyn & Darrel)

You update the site at **https://app.pagescms.org** – no code needed. Every save goes live on the website in about 1–2 minutes.

## Logging in
1. Open the invite email from Pages CMS and click the link (first time only).
2. After that, go to https://app.pagescms.org, sign in with the same email, and pick the **East Texas Adrenaline** site.
3. The menu on the left lists what you can edit: News, Gigs, Photo Gallery, Merch, Band Members, Releases.

## Post news
1. Click **News** → **Add an entry** (the + button).
2. Fill in **Title**, **Date**, and the **Story**. To add a photo, click **Photo** and upload one from your phone or computer.
3. Click **Save**. The newest post shows first.

## Add a gig
1. Click **Gigs** → **+**.
2. Pick the **Date**, then type the **Venue**, **City / address**, and any bands playing **With** you.
3. Not sure of the exact date yet? Pick the 1st of the month, type something like `March 2027 (TBA)` in **Date text**, and tick **Date not confirmed (TBA)**.
4. **Save**. After the date passes, the gig moves to "Past" by itself.

## Upload photos to the gallery
1. Click **Photo Gallery** → **+**.
2. Click **Photo** → upload. Bigger than about 2000 px wide? Shrink it first if you can, so the site stays fast.
3. Add a **Caption** and a short description of what's in the picture.
4. **Save**.

## Merch, band members, releases
Same idea: open the section, change the text or photo, **Save**. For merch, paste the exact product page link and the vendor's name, and only list prices exactly as the vendor shows them. Untick "Show Buy button" for items that aren't on sale online yet.

## Oops?
Every change is saved in history. If something breaks, message Sam/Grok Bot and it can be rolled back.
Please don't post anything about a show or product until it's confirmed.
